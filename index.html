Rank = weighted average of the Underdog and Redraft chips on each row. Chips never move; Rank slides between them.
Reading the bars. Every row shares one axis, pinned to the ruler at the top: the black line is your pick, the same spot on every row. Picks earlier than yours are to the left, later to the right, and each tick is one round (12 picks). A player's bar spans from his Underdog price to his Redraft price. Green, left of the line = the market usually takes him before your pick, so he's fallen to you; the number is how many picks of value. Red, right of the line = he usually goes later, so taking him now is a reach by that many picks. Gray straddling the line = fair. An arrow means he's more than two rounds off the edge.
Rank is the blend of the two prices. All view groups by the round each player should go in; position views group by tier (WR and TE pooled). Orange rows are likely gone before your next pick — orange + green bar means take him now. On the clock, TOP PICK is the best fit for your roster and VALUE is the best player who's fallen.
